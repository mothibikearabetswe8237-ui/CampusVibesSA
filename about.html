Our Background

Campus Vibes SA is a fictional student-focused organisation created for this academic project.

The organisation was created to improve interaction and socialisation among college and university students.

Students often receive information through WhatsApp groups, social media and word-of-mouth. This can make it difficult to find important information quickly.

Campus Vibes SA brings information about events, sports, games and student deals together in one place.

Our Mission and Vision

Area Information
Mission Assist students in finding opportunities, social interactions and activities that enhance campus life.
Vision Establish a reliable online platform that connects students with their school community.

Our Target Audience

Our main target audience is college and university students, especially first-year students.

  • First-year students
  • College students
  • University students
  • Students interested in sports
  • Students interested in social activities
